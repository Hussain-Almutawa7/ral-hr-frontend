RAL brand palette
=================

Core
  Ink       #2B1B3D    primary: backgrounds, headings, buttons
  Lavender  #D1C4E9    accent: text and highlights on dark
  Mid       #59476B    body text on white
  Soft      #7F6D91    labels, captions, hints
  Card      #F5F2F9    panel and callout backgrounds
  Rule      #DBD1E8    borders and separators

Status (app only, always paired with a word or an icon)
  Green     #1F7A54    approved, verified, on time, paid
  Amber     #A8631B    pending, late, expiring soon, needs review
  Red       #A32F2F    rejected, absent, expired, failed validation

Type
  Latin     SF Pro Display, or Inter as the free substitute
  Arabic    Noto Sans Arabic (shipped in brand/fonts/)
  Numbers   tabular figures, always, so money lines up in a column

CSS starting point
  :root{
    --ink:#2B1B3D; --lav:#D1C4E9; --mid:#59476B;
    --soft:#7F6D91; --card:#F5F2F9; --rule:#DBD1E8;
    --good:#1F7A54; --warn:#A8631B; --stop:#A32F2F;
  }
